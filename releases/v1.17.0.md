A smaller download for your vault. The main APK now ships separately from the Office reader. On your first Word, Excel, PowerPoint or other LibreOffice file, tap **Install reader**. MyDocuVault selects the signed add-on for your phone. After setup, Office files open offline from the vault.

Turn on **Release notifications** in Settings to see one short release highlight when a new version is available. Tap **Open update** to return to the vault or **What’s new** to read the release notes.

For a manual install, choose **MyDocuVault-v1.17.0.apk**. The four files named **MyDocuVault-Reader** are optional processor-specific Office readers.
