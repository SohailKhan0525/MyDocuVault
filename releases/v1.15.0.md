### Swipe back to preview

In the vault and setup guide, a supported Android back gesture reveals the previous screen as you swipe. Release to go back, or slide your finger back to the edge to stay. The toolbar and system Back button also work.

### A guide that fits the screen

Papers settle into their folder, the bookmark slides into place, and the lock closes as each guide page opens. Illustrations play once and respect the system animation setting. On wider windows, the illustration and instructions sit side by side. Narrow windows keep the guide controls in reach.

Install **MyDocuVault-v1.15.0.apk** over your existing copy to keep your vault.
