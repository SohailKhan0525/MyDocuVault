### Read Office files inside your vault

Word documents, spreadsheets, presentations and OpenDocument files open directly in the bundled LibreOffice reader. Both saved files and Android’s **Open with** use the same offline reader. The universal APK contains engines for ARM64, ARMv7, x86-64 and x86.

### Find your way around

An illustrated first-run guide takes you from filing a document to setting your PIN. Returning users go directly to unlock. The filing cabinet uses a quieter layout, and source files have syntax highlighting.

### Install or update

Download **MyDocuVault-v1.14.0.apk** on Android 8.0 or later. Install it over your existing copy to keep your vault. The adjacent SHA-256 file lets you verify the download.
